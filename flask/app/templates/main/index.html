How the relay works

The National Animal Rescue Network is a coordination layer — not a shelter. We partner with overlooked shelters and animal control centers in underserved areas, match dogs with vetted fosters and adopters, and move them along multi-leg transport routes to where they have a real chance.

Every dog you see on this site cleared a review. Every foster and transport volunteer is verified. Every partner shelter agreed to our standards. The relay only works when every link holds.
