# Homepage hero asset

`narn-homepage-hero.png` was created with the built-in image-generation tool for this project. It is 1983 × 793 pixels and designed for a wide Google Sites banner with copy on the left.

## Final generation prompt

Use case: photorealistic-natural

Asset type: wide Google Sites homepage hero banner

Primary request: Create an authentic documentary-style photograph representing a national animal rescue network.

Scene/backdrop: an airy animal shelter courtyard in soft early-morning light, subtle depth, clean and hopeful but not staged.

Subject: a diverse small group of adult rescue volunteers standing and kneeling with several healthy mixed-breed rescue dogs of varied sizes and coat colors; gentle, safe, natural interactions; dogs are calm and visibly cared for.

Style/medium: high-end nonprofit editorial photography, realistic anatomy and fur, candid emotion, inclusive and trustworthy.

Composition/framing: extra-wide landscape composition suitable for a responsive website banner; group weighted to the right half; generous uncluttered negative space on the left for a Google Sites headline overlay; important faces and dogs remain within the central safe area for mobile cropping.

Lighting/mood: warm natural light, hopeful, capable, community-centered, humane.

Color palette: warm cream, muted teal accents, natural green, soft rust and golden tones.

Constraints: no text, no logos, no branded clothing, no cages dominating the scene, no sadness or distress, no visible injuries, no exaggerated smiles, no watermark. Avoid duplicated animals, malformed paws, extra limbs, uncanny faces, or overly glossy stock-photo styling.
